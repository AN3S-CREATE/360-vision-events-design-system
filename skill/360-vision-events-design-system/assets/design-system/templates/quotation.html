01Brief and commercial terms.

This proposal covers the full production of a 600-guest outdoor corporate function for [Client company] on [Date of event] at [Venue]. One team runs the floor from load-in to strike, on one invoice.

The scope takes in the tent structure and seating, sound reinforcement, presentation staging, power distribution from the venue supply, crew, freight and on-site production management.

Commercial terms & inclusions

Total commercial investment
R 220 000.00 · flat, all-inclusive, non-VAT.
Taxation terms
Quoted on an all-inclusive, non-VAT basis. No VAT is added.
Scope inclusions
Stretch tent structure, conference seating for 600 guests, fire safety equipment, line-array PA, digital mixing console, wireless microphones, presentation stage and lectern, power distribution, technical crew, freight and on-site production management.
Site exclusions / venue provisions
Grid power at the distribution point, guest ablutions, catering, décor and lighting are provided by the client or the venue, and are excluded unless quoted separately.

02Service and infrastructure specification.

A · Tentage & seating

Stretch tent structure
Two rigged stretch tents joined into one covered area, sized for 600 seated guests with clear aisles and exits.
Conference seating
600 conference chairs, cleaned and set out in theatre-style blocks.
Fire safety
Six serviced fire extinguishers, placed at the tent exits.
Rigging and freight
Rigging crew for build, show standby and strike, with freight for delivery and collection.

B · Technical production, audio & staging

Line-array PA
Line-array speakers with subwoofers, tuned for speech and background music across the tent.
Mixing and control
Digital mixing console with stage box and signal processing.
Wireless microphones
Four wireless channels — handheld and lapel microphones with receivers.
Stage and lectern
Modular presentation stage with stairs and skirting, and a presenter lectern.
Power distribution
Distribution boards and cabling from the venue supply point.
Technical crew
Sound engineer and system technicians for the full production cycle.

C · Production staff & event management

  • Production manager — 3 days on site.
  • Operations manager — 3 days on site.
  • Logistics vehicles for crew and equipment.
  • Event management and turnkey coordination.
